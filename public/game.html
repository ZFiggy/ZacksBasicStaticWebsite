Controls & scoring

Hold arrows / WASD or the direction buttons to run. Space / Wait lets a turn pass; P pauses. Rival defenders chase your new position after every step and take a second move on burst turns. Only a chaser sharing your exact tile causes a tackle, whether you run into it or it reaches you after your move. Standing next to a chaser is safe. Stationary rival blockers only block movement; they do not take the ball. Your blockers keep chasers out of the outlined green tiles: their own tile and four neighbors, excluding diagonals. Next-move labels include both moves of a burst. SAFE means this turn only. Running out of steps ends the drive and changes possession even without contact. Collect the football and score in the right end zone before your steps run out. Every step spends 100 bonus points. Match the shortest route for 3 stars; finish within 2 extra steps for 2 stars. Coach hints cost 250 points each and cap the award at 1 star. On a turnover, the rival recovers the football and returns it to the left end zone. Drag the field to scout ahead on phones, or use Full field for an overview.
