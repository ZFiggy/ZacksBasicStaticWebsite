The playbook

Each kick assigns a different spot. Read the hash mark, then set your aim and arc. Read the rush and choose which lane to protect. Snap, lock enough power to reach the posts, then steer the aiming dot into the green zone with Left/Right or A/D and release before the rush arrives. Crosswinds shift during the play; headwinds reduce range and tailwinds add distance. High arcs beat the block but sacrifice range. Each field goal scores 3 points.

Use the button or press Space while the field is focused. Left/Right or A/D steer after power is locked; touch players can hold the aim buttons. Five attempts per round.
